1. What Stampyard does

Stampyard suggests which postage stamp is shown in your photo and shows similar stamps and, where available, current listing prices. Answers marked "✓ Verified match" were matched against a reference image. Other answers are AI suggestions and may be wrong.

2. Identifications and prices are estimates

Stamp identification and value are hard. Small differences (perforation, watermark, shade, printing) can change a stamp's identity and price a lot, and the app can't detect all of them from a photo. Prices shown are current eBay asking prices for similar listings, not appraisals or offers. Don't rely on Stampyard alone to buy, sell, insure or authenticate stamps. For valuable items, consult an expert or an expertising service.
